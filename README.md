Tradedge Labs
A trading journal, analytics, and real-time market scanning platform built for day traders.
Tradedge brings trade history, performance analytics, automated market scanning, and risk monitoring into one workspace. Connect brokerage accounts via API or import CSV files to understand your performance, while the automated scanner detects unusual market moves as they happen.

Real-Time Market Scanner
Tradedge’s automated scanner monitors the market in real time to detect unusual price movements and trading activity. It surfaces stocks making abnormal moves so you can identify potential setups and investigate what’s driving the action.
Trading Journal
Review your results by day, ticker, and account. Use your trading history to identify patterns, evaluate your decisions, and build a more consistent review process.
P&L Calendar
See your performance at a glance with:
- Monthly profit and loss
- Win rate and profit factor
- A color-coded daily P&L calendar
- Weekly totals

Multiple Accounts
Review several brokerage accounts together or focus on one account at a time.

- Trade direction

Market Overview
Bring broader market context into your research and daily trading workflow.

Market Event Calendar

Automated Risk Rules
Monitor open positions with automated risk rules to support a more structured approach to risk management.

The Workflow
1. Connect your accounts — Link a brokerage account via API or import your trades from a CSV file.
2. Scan the market — Detect unusual market moves in real time with the automated scanner.
3. Review market events — Add upcoming events to your research.
4. Monitor your positions — Use automated risk rules to support your trading process.
5. Analyze your results — Review daily P&L and break down performance by account, ticker, direction, and timing.
6. Refine your approach — Use patterns from your trading history to guide your next review.
